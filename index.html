sm3gm

Free mods and small tools for PC games.

I build modifications for games I play — mostly quality-of-life fixes and content additions that the base game left out. Everything here is free to download and free to use.

Independent hobby project, run by one person from Norway. No company behind it, no paid tiers, nothing locked away.
